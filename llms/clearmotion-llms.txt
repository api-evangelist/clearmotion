# ClearMotion

> ClearMotion is an automotive motion-technology company founded out of MIT in 2012 and headquartered in Billerica, Massachusetts, with engineering in Nuneaton (UK), an office in Shanghai and a plant in Changshu, China. Its CM1 fully active suspension system pairs the ActiValve digital hydraulic actuator with control software that cancels road disturbances in milliseconds, and RoadMotion is its crowdsourced cloud road-surface intelligence layer, mapping road features to better than 20mm and feeding them forward to suspension, chassis and ADAS subsystems.
